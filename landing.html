The honest number

Why 80% is the wrong target.

You can hit an 80% win-rate — but only with a target so tight the rare full stop cancels every small win. Push the target out and the win-rate falls while the expectancy climbs.

The real edge sits at ~50% wins and +0.48R a trade. High win-rate ≠ profit — and the terminal refuses to pretend otherwise.
